## মালিকের জন্য সংক্ষেপে (সহজ বাংলায়)

1. ব্লগই হবে গুগল থেকে লোক আনার মূল ইঞ্জিন। ৮টা বড় বিষয় ঠিক করেছি: কাপড়ের যত্ন, সাইজ ও ফিট, কাপড়ের ধরন, স্টাইল, উৎসব ও অনুষ্ঠান, আবহাওয়া, পণ্য ও ব্র্যান্ড গাইড, আর EXTAS কীভাবে কাপড় বানায় ও চেক করে।
2. প্রতিটা লেখা থেকে অন্তত একটা ক্যাটাগরি বা পণ্যের পেজে লিংক যাবে, যাতে যে পড়তে আসে সে কিনতেও পারে।
3. ১৭৯টা টপিকের তালিকা বানিয়েছি: ১৬৭টা নতুন, আর ডেমোর ১২টা পুরনো লেখা নতুন করে লেখা হবে। সব টপিক গুগলে মানুষ সত্যি যা লিখে খোঁজে, সেখান থেকে নেওয়া। কতজন খোঁজে সেই সংখ্যা আমরা বানাইনি।
4. প্রথম ২ সপ্তাহ দিনে ৩টা পোস্ট, তারপর দিনে ৫টা। দিনে ৩টার হিসাব পুরো ৯০ দিন এই তালিকা দিয়েই চলবে। ৫টা পর্যন্ত বাকি পোস্ট আসবে প্রতি মাসে নতুন টপিক যোগ করে।
5. এখন শীত, তাই শুরু হবে হুডি, জ্যাকেট, সোয়েটার দিয়ে। ২০২৭ সালের ঈদুল ফিতর সম্ভবত ১০ মার্চ (চাঁদ দেখার ওপর নির্ভর করে)। তাই ঈদের লেখাগুলো ১০ জানুয়ারির মধ্যে প্রকাশ হবে।
6. লিখবে Doot, আর প্রুফ দেখা ও তথ্য যাচাই করবে Gyani (প্রধান এজেন্ট)। কেউ নিজের লেখা নিজে পাস করতে পারবে না।
7. বেশি পোস্ট দিলেই গুগল বেশি দেখায় না। কম মানের অনেক লেখা দিলে উল্টো শাস্তি হয়। তাই প্রতিটা লেখায় থাকবে নিজেদের ছবি, নিজেদের টেস্ট, যাচাই করা তথ্য। আগের কোনো লেখার সাথে বেশি মিল থাকলে লেখাটা আটকে যাবে।

9. আপনার কাছে একটা জিনিস লাগবে: সপ্তাহে আধা দিনের জন্য একজন স্টাফ, যে কাপড় ধুয়ে, মেপে, ছবি তুলবে। এতে লেখায় সত্যি "আমরা নিজেরা টেস্ট করেছি" বলা যাবে।
10. টপিকের তালিকা আপনি ব্যাকএন্ডে দেখে অনুমোদন দেবেন, চাইলে নিজের টপিকও যোগ করবেন। এর বাইরে মানের দায়িত্ব পুরোটা আমাদের।
11. প্রতিটা পোস্ট থেকে কতজন এলো আর কত বিক্রি হলো, সেই রিপোর্ট প্রতি মাসে পাবেন। যে লেখা কাজ করে না, সেটা আপডেট হবে বা অন্য লেখার সাথে মিশিয়ে দেওয়া হবে।
12. একটা সিদ্ধান্ত আপনার: লেখার নিচে লেখকের নাম কী হবে। প্রস্তাব: "EXTAS Journal" নামে। যেখানে আপনি বা আপনার টিমের কেউ সত্যি মতামত দেবেন, সেখানে তার নাম যাবে।

## 1. Topic architecture: 8 pillars, each with a hub and clusters

**Shape [HOUSE, on the OFFICIAL basis that internal links help Google understand a site and every page needs a link]:** each pillar has one **hub page** at `/journal/<pillar-slug>`. A hub is a full guide in its own right: it summarises the pillar, then lists every cluster post as an auto-updated section. Each **cluster** is a group of posts on one sub-theme. Every post links up to its hub and out to at least one EXTAS category or product.

Category slugs below follow the traffic plan §1.4. Some categories are not in the demo yet (denim, jackets, sweaters, sweatshirts, joggers, tank tops, caps, boxers, shackets, jerseys, top-bottom sets, katua). Slugs are owner-configurable (PR 590); the topic bank uses the proposed defaults.

**Why these eight [HOUSE]:** P1–P3 answer the everyday-knowledge questions the owner named (keeping a polo in shape, washing denim, fabrics, fit). P4–P6 are styling, occasions and seasons, and they carry the seasonal demand. P7 turns readers into buyers. P8 carries the trust signals (who, how, why) that make the other seven believable. Help articles (`/help/delivery`, `/help/exchange`) stay in S12 PR 247–248. They are linked from P8, not duplicated.

**Cannibalisation rule (from the traffic plan, D03 §3) [HOUSE]:** one primary query per URL **across the whole site**. Price and "buy" queries ("polo shirt price in bd", "t shirt combo price in bd") belong to category and combo pages, **never** to a journal post. Journal posts target the informational twin ("how to choose a polo shirt", "polo combo vs single") and link to the category.

**Limits.**
- **"People also ask" could not be observed.** Our search tool returns links, not the PAA box. Question phrasing comes from autocomplete instead.
- The probe server is outside Bangladesh, so some suggestions leaked Indian or Western results. Those were dropped, or are marked where kept.
- Autocomplete proves a phrasing exists, **not how many people search it**. No volumes are given.
- **[HOUSE]** in the query cell marks the few topics chosen from first-hand knowledge without an autocomplete hit. Each of those must show demand in Search Console within 90 days, or it is merged (§8).

**Columns.**
- **Intent:** Info = learn · Info→Comm = learn, then likely buy · Comm = choosing what to buy · Nav = about EXTAS.
- **BN twin:** a natively written `/bn/journal/…` twin behind the translation gate (PR 588). "Bengali first" = the main post is written in Bengali, and an English twin is optional.
- **Type:** feeds the calendar mix.
- **Week:** the publishing week in §3. "After day 90" = seasonal, outside this window.

**Totals:** 125 with a Bengali twin + 9 Bengali-first · 8 pillar hubs, 82 evergreen, 54 seasonal, 20 product/brand, 15 comparisons · 16 fall after day 90 (monsoon, summer, Boishakh, Puja, Eid ul-Adha). ## 4. Interlinking rules

**Basis [OFFICIAL]:** "Every page you care about should have a link from at least one other page on your site." Good anchor text is "descriptive, reasonably concise, and relevant". Avoid "click here". "Resist the urge to cram every keyword" into link text. Google discovers links only when they are `<a>` elements with an `href` (Link best practices, updated 2025-12-10; traffic plan §1.7).

### 4.1 Links every post must carry (3–5 contextual links in the body; automatic blocks don't count)

| # | Link | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Cluster → its hub** | Exactly 1, in the first third of the post, anchor = the hub's topic ("our full care guide", "men's size guide for Bangladesh") |
| L2 | **Post → category or product** | 1–2. To the category the post is about, with a descriptive anchor ("men's hoodies", "pique polo shirts"), never "shop now" alone. Product posts link the product. **Style posts that show a set link the combo page, not single pieces.** A reader who arrives through a combo is never sent to a single product page (lessons/ALL.md) |
| L3 | **Post → sibling post** | 1–2 links to posts in the same cluster, where the reader would really want them next (e.g. "collar curling" → "iron a polo") |
| L4 | **Hub → every cluster post** | Automatic list section on the hub, newest first within each cluster. This also stops orphan posts (§4.3) |
| L5 | **Product page → care guide** | Every PDP shows "How to care for this" linking to the matching P1 post, mapped by fabric and category (e.g. pique polo → P1-01; denim → P1-05; hoodie → P1-07). Fallback: the care hub (B5) |
| L6 | **Journal → combos** | Every P4 outfit post and the weekly lookbook carry a "Shop the look" block linking the relevant combo (`/combos/<rule>` or a featured pair). Combo pages link back to 1–2 matching style posts |
| L7 | **Category page → guides** | Each category page shows a "Guides" strip with up to 3 posts linked to that category (size chart, care, buying guide) |
| L8 | **Language twin** | A post with a Bengali twin links its twin with a plain `<a href>` (traffic plan §1.1). Bengali posts link Bengali hubs and `/bn/` categories |

**Anchor rules [HOUSE on OFFICIAL basis]:**
- Every anchor says what is behind it.
- The same target URL is linked at most once per post.
- Anchors vary naturally; the exact-match query is not repeated across posts.
- No links inside headings.
- No more than one link per 100 words of body text.

### 4.2 Back-links when a post goes live (so older posts point to new ones)

On publish, the system suggests **2 older posts** in the same cluster or pillar where a sentence can link to the new post. The proofreader approves the sentence. The edit is small and does **not** change the older post's `dateModified` (only substantive changes do, §5.5).

### 4.3 Orphan prevention

| Layer | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Publish gate | A post cannot publish unless it has L1 and L2, and its hub list (L4) will include it on publish |
| 7-day task | Within 7 days it must have at least 2 contextual links in from other posts (§4.2). If not, it goes on the proofreader's queue |
| Crawl paths | Journal index with paginated `<a href>` links, pillar index pages, category "Guides" strips (L7), XML journal sitemap per language (PR 228) |
| Weekly orphan report | Any indexable post with fewer than 2 internal links in → fix task; target **0 orphans** (B4) |

### 4.4 Related-posts block (server-rendered `<a href>`, 3 posts)

Picked in this order:
1. Two posts from the same cluster.
2. One from the same pillar or the same linked category.

Filters and tie-breaks:
- Same language as the reader.
- Never a post already linked in the body.
- Never an out-of-season seasonal post (unless it is evergreen).
- Ties go to the post with **fewer** internal links in, so link value spreads instead of piling onto winners.

The output is the same on every render (no random order), so Google sees a stable page [HOUSE].

## 5. Quality system (ours to guarantee)

### 5.1 Writer → proofreader / fact-checker → publish

```
Topic bank (owner approves the list; PR 591)
  → Brief (PR 528 form: target query, intent, outline, evidence needed, links L1–L3, photo shot list)
  → Doot drafts (EN, or BN for Bengali-first)            ── automatic checks (5.2 A) must pass
  → Proofreader + fact-checker: Gyani A360-01             ── human-style review (5.2 B): PASS / FIX / KILL
       FIX → back to Doot, at most 2 rounds, then KILL or park
  → Scheduler queue (buffer of at least 10 approved posts)
  → Publish at slot → sitemap ping → back-link task (4.2) → social share (S23) for 1–2 posts a day
  → Day 28 and day 90 review (§8)
```

**Proposed proofreader: Gyani (জ্ঞানী, A360-01).** Reasons:
1. **Independence.** The writer never approves its own post (PR 591). Gyani is the chief who already grades the other agents (PR 583), so its pass/fail verdicts feed Doot's appraisal without a conflict of interest.
2. **The facts live in its stages.** Gyani owns **Smart Rules (S10)**, the source for delivery, exchange and price lines; **support (S22)**, which knows what customers ask; and the **photoshoot studio (S24)**, which produces the images it will check.
3. **Its rights fit the job.** It can read every agent's memory (PR 582), so it can check that a post agrees with what other agents know (e.g. Bhandari's catalogue facts).

**Why not Shilpi as the main proofreader:** Shilpi owns the design layer and "never touches business logic". Fact-checking policy and product claims is business logic. Shilpi **does** do a monthly image audit on a 10% sample (own-photo rule, alt text, set photos), because it holds the alt-text and photo skills (shilpi/photo/06).

**Why not Bhandari:** it owns the product facts but "never touches marketing copy". Its catalogue is the **automatic** source the checks read, and it is not a reviewer.

**Model independence [HOUSE]:** under PR 580, the proofreading job uses a different qualified model from the drafting job where both pass the quality bar, so the two do not share the same blind spots (B13).

### 5.2 The checklist

**A. Automatic checks (block on fail) [HOUSE]**

| Check | Rule |
|---|---|
| Similarity / duplicate topic | (1) **Query registry:** the post's primary query is not owned by any other URL (posts, categories, combos, help). (2) **Title match:** normalised title token overlap ≥ 0.6 with any existing or queued post → flag. (3) **Body similarity:** embedding cosine ≥ 0.85 against any published or queued post (either language) → **block**; 0.75–0.85 → the proofreader must write down the distinct intent or merge. (4) Optional **SERP overlap:** if 4 or more of the top-10 URLs for two posts' queries are the same, treat them as one intent and merge [COMMUNITY]. Basis: Mueller, "Reduce unnecessary duplication and spend your energy on a fantastic page", and pages "aren't duplicates just because they happen to appear in the same search results page" (SEJ, Sept 2025) [COMMUNITY: reported official view] |
| Source-copy check | For PR 529 posts and any post drafted from a reference: no passage of 12 or more consecutive words matches a source, and overall similarity to each source is under the B12 threshold |
| Links | L1 + L2 present; 3–5 contextual links; no broken or redirected internal links; anchors not generic |
| Product block | Products pulled live from the catalogue (price, stock, name, brand field), never typed by the writer; a sold-out product swaps automatically (B10) |
| Policy lines | Delivery, exchange and payment statements come from Smart Rules tokens, never typed |
| Banned claims | No invented numbers, reviews or quotes; no "best in Bangladesh"; no "100% original" or "official" without proof on file; no medical or skin claims; no competitor disparagement |
| Readability | **EN:** median sentence ≤ 20 words; Flesch reading ease ≥ 60 as a guide [COMMUNITY]. **BN:** চলতি only (flags সাধু verb forms such as করিয়াছে / হইয়াছে); sentences ≤ about 20 words; plain words (B14) |
| SEO technical | One H1; meta title and description lengths (§6); slug rule; hero image present with alt text; `BlogPosting` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD valid and matching the visible text |
| Images | At least 1 own photo (catalogue, care lab or studio) per post; alt text present in the page's language; descriptive filename; no image from Google image search (PR 547) |

### 5.4 Safety valve: automatic throttle [HOUSE] (B7)

The scheduler **drops from 5 to 3 posts a day**, tells the owner why in Bengali, and goes back up only when the condition clears for 2 weeks, if any of these happen:
- The gate first-pass rate falls below 70% over the last 7 days.
- Any factual error is reported after publishing.
- Fewer than 60% of journal URLs older than 28 days are indexed (Search Console Pages report or the URL Inspection API).
- Search Console shows a manual action, or a Google spam/core update is in progress (Search Status Dashboard). In that case we hold at 3/day until the rollout ends and 2 weeks of data are seen (LEARNING-LOOP trigger).

It **pauses publishing completely** on a manual action.

### 5.5 Refreshing old posts

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 28 / day 90 review: average position 8–20 | Improve: answer the query more directly, add evidence or a photo, fix the title or meta for CTR |
| Clicks down at least 30% versus the previous 28 days (seasonality checked against last year) | Refresh content; check whether a competitor now answers better |
| Year-bound topic (Eid, winter, collections) | Refresh the **same URL** about 8 weeks before the season; new year in the visible title only |
| Product block shows retired products, broken links, a changed policy | Fixed automatically where possible (B10); otherwise a refresh task |
| Two posts swap places for one query (Search Console) | Merge into the stronger URL and 301 the other (PR 235, B6) |

**Date rule [OFFICIAL basis]:** `dateModified` and the visible "Updated" date change only when the main content substantially changed. A small edit, a link insert or a typo fix does not count. The system compares the size of the text change before it allows a date change (B6).

### 5.6 E-E-A-T on the page

- **Author:** "EXTAS Journal" (Organization) linked to an editorial-policy / about-the-journal page: who we are (an apparel business and nominated supplier exporting menswear, which is real experience), how posts are made, who checks them, how to report an error.

- **Evidence box** in every care or test post: "How we know" (care-lab date, what was washed and how, before/after photos and measurements).
- **Trust links:** exchange policy, contact, delivery (P8 and help pages).

### 7.2 Throughput check [EST]

A day at 5/day ≈ 3–4 new English posts and 1–2 Bengali twins. Agent time is compute, not staff hours. The real limits are:
1. **The review queue.** Each post gets a full review; at 5–6 posts a day this is within one reviewer agent's daily capacity [EST].
2. **Photos.**
3. **Real topics.** The bank covers the 3/day minimum for 90 days; 5/day needs about 154 more topics by W13, so the monthly expansion must add about 50–60 approved topics a month.

If any limit is hit, the throttle (§5.4) holds 3/day.

### 7.4 Scheduling through the day

- 3/day: 09:00 · 13:00 · 19:00. 5/day: 08:00 · 11:00 · 14:00 · 17:00 · 20:00 (Bangladesh time) [HOUSE].

### 7.5 Cost note (PR 580, PR 309, PR 312–313)

**Job types**, each with its own quality test set under PR 580: topic research · brief · English draft · Bengali draft (native, not translation) · proofread and fact-check · meta and alt text · similarity embedding.

**Model choice:** the system suggests the **cheapest model that passes each job's quality bar**.
- Drafting and proofreading stay on models that pass the full test set.
- Meta/alt and embeddings can use cheaper qualified models.
- **The fact-check job is never moved to a model that failed its test set**, to save money or for any other reason.

**Volume [EST]:** about 60–90k tokens per English post (reading sources and top results, brief, draft, self-check, review, meta) and about 25–35k per Bengali twin. That comes to roughly 8–12 million tokens a month at 5/day. Money figures come from the live cost book (PR 312–313) at the chosen models' current prices; Doot does not quote prices here. The per-agent spend cap (PR 309) applies; at 80% of the cap the owner is warned, and at 100% the journal falls to 3/day rather than stopping. Images are the bigger variable cost if AI generation is used, so it needs permission each time.
