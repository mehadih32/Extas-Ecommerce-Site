# EXTAS demo store — QA report, round R15

- **Build tested:** `scratchpad/proto/dist/qa-snap.html` (1,686,782 bytes, 2026-10-19 04:21). Source read-only (`proto/src`, nothing edited).
- **Devices:** Chromium phones 320×640, 360×800, 375×667, 393×852, 430×932, 440×956 (isMobile + touch, real pixel ratio); desktop 1280×800, 1440×900.
- **WebKit / iPhone engine: NOT tested** — only Chromium is installed (`/opt/pw-browsers` has chromium-1194 only; installing is not allowed). Anything that depends on Safari's own swipe-back, its page snapshot, or its scroll restoration is marked "needs a real iPhone".
- **Gestures:** edge swipe = CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` from x=5 to x=180 (≈270–350 ms); pull-to-refresh = CDP touch drag 300 px down from y=170; browser Back/Forward = `page.goBack()/goForward()`; Backspace; Alt+←/→.
- **How jolts were measured:** a recorder injected before the app logs, after every hash change, `scrollY`, `main.page` top, first heading top, header top and text length at 0/50/150/300/500/800/1500 ms, plus Chromium `layout-shift` entries (CLS) and every scroll event. Scroll restore = scrollY just before leaving vs. scrollY 1.6 s after returning.
- **Scripts & raw data:** `scratchpad/qa-r15-tools/` (`journeys.js`, `matrix.js`, `features.js`, `probe2.js`, `probe3.js`, `swipe-rel.js`; JSON in `out/`). Screenshots: `scratchpad/qa-r15/`.
