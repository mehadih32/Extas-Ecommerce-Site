## 1. Trust [RESEARCH: NN/g + Baymard]
NN/g's trust & credibility research covers: professional design and working search, company information, expert product
information, robust reviews, clear pricing, respectful data requests, performance/errors, security and policies, support.
Baymard: 19% abandon because they distrust the site with card details; 17% because of errors.

EXTAS trust block (near Buy and in checkout) **[HOUSE]**:
- COD everywhere in Bangladesh · delivery time per band · exchange rule in one plain line (from Smart Rules) · WhatsApp/hotline with hours · real address/trade licence in footer · verified-buyer reviews with fit and photos.
- Show **real** stock/urgency only ("এই সাইজে আর ২টা আছে" only when true).
- Reviews: only delivered buyers; owner approves (blueprint); never generated (standing rule). Show negatives too — NN/g: mixed reviews build credibility; shoppers often read negative reviews first.

## 2. Returns / exchange
- EXTAS rule (Rules Center): **no return after receipt, exchange only; exchange delivery charge applies; free if our mistake** (photo required); door refusal = pay delivery charge only.
- Baymard flags hard-to-find return policies on product pages as an "essential, low-cost" fix (guideline 803; details paywalled) [RESEARCH].
- Rules **[HOUSE]**: state the exchange rule on the PDP (near Buy), in the bag and at checkout, in the same words; link to the full policy; explain *how* to exchange in 3 steps; never surprise a customer at the door.
- Returns-reduction levers that leaders use: size advice and fit data. Zalando reported that items with its size advice had **10% fewer size-related returns** than items without (press release, Jul 2023) [VENDOR-REPORTED]. EXTAS equivalents: "Find my size", fit bar from reviews, model size info.

## 3. Pricing psychology (what the evidence supports)
| Tactic | Evidence | EXTAS use |
|---|---|---|
| Price endings (৳..9 / ৳..99) | Anderson & Simester (2003, QME): $9 endings raised demand in all three field experiments; stronger for new items/limited-information products [ACADEMIC] | test ৳999/৳1,499 vs ৳1,000/৳1,500 on new styles — owner approves every price |
| Bundle saving framing | Stremersch & Tellis: one bundle price lowers price sensitivity; discounts better shown as several separate savings [ACADEMIC] | "৳351 বাঁচবে" + per-piece price |
| Middle option | compromise effect common in markets (Huber et al. 2014) [ACADEMIC] | highlight middle tier |
| Decoys | rare/fragile in real markets (same) | don't build fake decoys |
| Strikethrough "was" price | must be a real former price (Google merchant listing rules for strikethrough; consumer-protection basics) [OFFICIAL] | only from price history |
| Total cost transparency | 12% abandon when total isn't visible upfront (Baymard) [RESEARCH] | show delivery charge on PDP and bag |
| Free-delivery threshold | must be near Buy, conditions clear, updated by bag content (Baymard) [RESEARCH] | "আর ৳X কিনলে ফ্রি ডেলিভারি" |

All price changes, discounts and thresholds are on the **approval list** — Sawdagor proposes with data, the owner decides.
