# S06 · Lessons from leading fashion e-commerce players (adapted to EXTAS)

*Sawdagor সওদাগর · A360-04 · module 6 of 6 · level: PhD (judgement) · version 1.0 · as of October 2026*

Learn the **mechanism**, not the brand's numbers — most figures below are self-reported by the companies.

| Player / source | What they do | Evidence | EXTAS adaptation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zalando** | size recommendation from two phone photos (body measurements), plus earlier size-advice tools | press release Jul 2023: items with size advice had **10% fewer size-related returns** (company-reported) | "Find my size" from height/weight + fit-from-reviews bar; measure refusal-for-size rate before/after |
| **ASOS** | "Fit Assistant" — ML size recommendations from past purchases/returns + 3 optional questions (2018) | launch coverage; **no** published impact figure | same input idea: past orders + exchanges teach our size advice |
| **Baymard-benchmarked apparel leaders** | size buttons, model measurements, reviewer photos browsable, fit subscore | 90% of apparel sites miss ≥ 1 of 5 practices | S01 §3 checklist |
| **Fabrilife (BD competitor, our teardown)** | huge catalogue (2,176 products), every product discounted, full-screen promo modal each new session, slow pages (LCP 11–14 s measured) | teardown-fabrilife.md (measured 17 Oct 2026) | win on speed, clarity, honest prices, no entry popups, combos done properly |
| **Large DTC brands' growth teams** (via CTC) | plan by contribution margin; daily targets; alerts at > 10% off plan | CTC Prophit System (agency) | Gyani's KPI tree + Tirondaj's budget rules |
| **Indian D2C (COD market)** | COD still ~46% of orders; QR-at-door payments; partial-COD deposits by some | Shopflo report via YourStory (Dec 2024) | keep COD hero; deposits only as owner decision |

## Patterns worth copying (ranked)
1. **Fit certainty** is the #1 fashion lever (sizing info, fit data, size advice) — it lifts conversion *and* cuts size refusals/exchanges.
2. **Outfits, not items** — sets and complete-the-look raise AOV (S02).
3. **Honest, total pricing** up front — delivery charge visible early.
4. **Speed and simplicity on phones** — the local competitor benchmark is slow; that's our opening.
5. **Measured experimentation culture** — every rule proven with our own data (CURRICULUM: PhD level designs EXTAS's conversion playbook and proves each rule).

## Patterns not to copy
- Permanent "sale" on every product (erodes trust; strikethrough must be real).
- Entry popups blocking the first screen.
- Free returns economics of EU giants — EXTAS's rule is exchange-only; don't import policies that the owner hasn't approved.
