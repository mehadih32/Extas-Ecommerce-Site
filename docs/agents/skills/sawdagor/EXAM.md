## Answer key
1. Extra costs too high, 40% → show delivery charge on PDP/bag early, total before confirm.
2. 11.3; 8.
3. 42% of testers typed their full name into "First Name"; fewer fields, fewer errors.
4. Any five: conventional sizes; numeric sizes; cm + inches; international conversions; how to measure; product-specific chart; link near size selectors; back returns to PDP; support link; model measurements.
5. Buttons; disabled/greyed, not hidden.
6. Near the Buy section with conditions, updated by bag content; 27% missed banner-only info (banner blindness).
7. Mixed; sell sets and singles (outside the combo-only sections).
8. Product bundling (designed, integrated look) — adds value beyond the sum, less price-war driven.
9. Several separate savings rather than one lump discount (and a single bundle price lowers price sensitivity).
10. Compromise effect; highlight the middle tier, no fake decoys.
11. Graded: three tiers, per-piece price, taka saving, middle highlighted, prices marked as owner-approved placeholders.
12. Demand rose in all three field experiments; stronger for new items / limited-information products.
13. BD: > 90% prefer COD (e-CAB via US ITA guide, 2026); India: COD 45.6% of orders 2023–24 (Shopflo via YourStory).
14. No reliable source; vendor numbers are unsourced; use own data.
15. Any four: COD default hero; door rule at commitment; prepay nudges without punishment (owner approval); risk-based friction (advance charge for high risk, no auto-block); confirmation + 2-size check; accurate expectations; address resolver.
16. 62%; main navigation, search autocomplete, mobile forms, sitewide elements (any two).
17. 10% fewer size-related returns (company-reported); before/after or A/B on refusal-for-size and exchange-for-size rates.
18. e.g. "হাতে পাওয়ার পর রিটার্ন নেই, শুধু এক্সচেঞ্জ — এক্সচেঞ্জ ডেলিভারি চার্জ লাগবে; আমাদের ভুল হলে ফ্রি।" (must match Smart Rules wording)
19. Prices are owner decisions (he is asking, so it's his decision) — but check the strikethrough is a real former price (Google StrikethroughPrice rules/consumer protection), margin impact per style, combo interactions (better-of-two), stock; reply with impact numbers and the honest alternative if the "was" price isn't real.
20. Any three: no entry popup blocking the hero; fast pages (their LCP 11–14 s); honest pricing instead of permanent discounts; clean category labels (no raw slugs); clear combos.
