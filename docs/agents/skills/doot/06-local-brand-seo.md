## 1. Google Business Profile — is EXTAS eligible? [OFFICIAL]

- Only businesses that **make in-person contact with customers during stated hours** are eligible (shop-fronts and service-area businesses). **Online-only businesses are not eligible.**
- EXTAS today = online + COD delivery → **not eligible** unless it has a real place customers visit (showroom, pickup/exchange counter with hours, or a staffed office open to customers).
- If the owner opens a pickup/exchange point or showroom: create the profile with the real address and hours, category "Men's clothing store" (or the closest available), real photos, the website link, and keep hours current (Google's AI features doc also says keep Business Profile info current).
- **Never** create a profile at a fake address, virtual office or courier hub — suspension risk and against guidelines **[OFFICIAL basis: eligibility rules]**.
- Decision → BLOCKED.md for the owner: "Do we have, or want, a customer-facing location?" Recommended default: no GBP until there is one.

## 2. "Local" SEO for an online shop in Bangladesh (without GBP)

People search with places: "polo shirt in dhaka", "online shop bd", "chittagong delivery". Rules **[HOUSE]**:
- One clear **delivery & coverage page** (Dhaka city / outside Dhaka charges and times from Smart Rules; courier partners; exchange process) — that's what such queries need.
- **No city doorway pages** ("Polo shirt in Sylhet", "…in Khulna" with swapped city names) — Google's spam policy names "multiple domain names or pages targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page" as doorway abuse **[OFFICIAL]**.
- District-level content only when genuinely different (e.g. a real pickup point in Chattogram with its own address and hours).

## 3. Brand SERP (what people see when they search "EXTAS")

Goal: when someone searches "extas" or "extas bd", page 1 shows **our site, our social profiles, real reviews, no confusion**.
1. **Site name:** `WebSite` structured data on the home page with `name: "EXTAS"`, `alternateName` (lowercase domain); same name in title, `og:site_name`, header **[OFFICIAL]**.
2. **Organization / OnlineStore** markup with logo (≥ 112×112, visible on white), `sameAs` to Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok; `contactPoint`; telephone with +880 **[OFFICIAL]**.
3. Consistent **NAP-like facts** (name, phone/WhatsApp, email, policy wording) across site, Facebook page, Instagram bio, marketplace stores, press mentions **[COMMUNITY — standard practice]**.
4. Social profiles completed with the website link (they often rank for brand queries).
5. Sitelinks come from clear navigation and internal linking; they can't be set manually **[COMMUNITY — widely reported]**.
6. Watch for brand-name confusion (other "Extas" businesses) and brand + "fake" / "review" / "scam" queries in Search Console — answer with real content (policy, reviews page, contact).

## 4. Reviews and reputation
- On-site reviews only from verified delivered buyers (blueprint rule); AggregateRating markup only from those **[HOUSE + OFFICIAL review guidelines]**.
- Facebook page recommendations: reply to every negative one within 24 h, publicly and politely, then solve in DM (owner-approved reply templates; refunds/compensation need owner approval).
- Never buy or write reviews (EXTAS standing rule; also illegal/deceptive in many markets).

## 5. Brand demand is the long game
Branded search volume (Search Console brand queries, Google Trends for "extas") is the best single health metric for organic + paid working together. Report it monthly to Gyani.
