## Realism note
- **Realistic in days:** a new page discovered and indexed (internal links + sitemap + URL Inspection); structured data valid; a post published and answered.
- **Realistic in 2–6 weeks:** a long-tail EN/BN/Banglish query (low competition) in the top 10; first Merchant listing enhancements reported in Search Console.
- **Realistic in 3–6 months:** steady category rankings for mid-competition terms; first quality press links. (Shopify's SEO lead: most sites see measurable results in 3–6 months; competitive terms 12+ months.)
- **Not realistic:** top 3 for "polo shirt" or "t shirt bd" in weeks; guaranteed indexing of every URL; viral posts on demand.
