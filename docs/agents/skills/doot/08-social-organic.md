## 1. How the feeds rank (what Meta and Instagram have said)

- Meta ranks with many AI predictions of how valuable a post is to each person (e.g. likelihood to share or comment), combined with survey feedback; **no single prediction decides**. Meta published system cards for Feed, Stories, Reels and recommendations of unconnected content (June 2023) **[OFFICIAL, Meta newsroom]**.
- Instagram: no single algorithm — Feed, Stories, Explore and Reels each rank differently; Feed predicts time spent, comments, likes, saves, profile taps; Explore must also meet recommendation guidelines (Mosseri, 2021) **[OFFICIAL via SEJ report]**.
- **Sends (DM shares) per reach** called "one of the most important signals we use in ranking" by Adam Mosseri (mid-2024 Reel); 2025 reports say he named watch time, likes per reach and sends as top factors **[2-SRC reports of an official statement: Influencer Marketing Hub + Dataslayer]**.
- **Originality:** Meta (2025) reduces distribution of reposted/duplicate content and can remove monetisation for repeat reusers; transformative edits are allowed **[COMMUNITY-reported summary of a Meta announcement, Jul 2025]**.
- Unverified numbers circulating (e.g. "sends weighted 3–5× likes", "original content +40–60% reach", "10 reposts = excluded") have no primary source → do not quote **[REJECTED]**.

## 2. What that means for EXTAS posts

| Make content that… | Examples |
|---|---|
| people **send** to a friend | "Tag/send to the friend who always wears the same black tee"; combo-for-two ideas; Eid gift sets |
| people **watch to the end** | 7–15 s try-on transitions; "3 ways to wear one polo"; fabric test |
| people **save** | size chart carousel, care guide, outfit formulas |
| is **original** | own shoots, staff/customers (with consent), behind-the-scenes QC — no downloaded/watermarked reposts |

## 3. Weekly plan (starting cadence) [HOUSE — adjust from Insights after 6 weeks]

| Day | Facebook page | Instagram | Reuse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sat | new-arrival carousel (4:5) | same carousel | — |
| Sun | Reel: try-on / combo reveal | Reel | TikTok, YouTube Short |
| Tue | customer photo/review post (with permission) | Story + highlight | — |
| Wed | Reel: fabric/size help | Reel | TikTok, Short |
| Thu | offer/combo post (real offer) | Story with link sticker | WhatsApp channel (opt-in) |
| Daily | reply to every comment/message within 1 h in shop hours | same | — |

Rules:
1. Bengali first (চলতি), English product names; captions start with the hook; price and "COD all over Bangladesh" in the caption; link to the exact combo/category page with UTM.
2. Formats: 9:16 for Reels/Stories, 4:5 for feed (same shoot as Tirondaj's creative; Shilpi's framing rule — garment within central 4:5 area).
3. Comments are sales leads: answer price/size questions publicly in short, then DM details; never post customer phone numbers.
4. Posts that do well organically (high sends/saves) go to Tirondaj as ad candidates (creative lab T05).
5. Facebook Groups / community: share genuinely useful content (size help, care) in relevant groups only where rules allow; no spam drops.
6. Claims and prices must match the site today; no fake scarcity.

## 4. Metrics (Meta Business Suite / Instagram Insights)
- Per post: reach, **sends (shares) per reach**, saves per reach, watch time / average watch %, link clicks, profile visits.
- Per month: followers from non-follower reach, messages started, orders with UTM source = facebook/instagram organic (our DB).
- Kill/keep: formats with below-median sends/reach for 4 straight posts are replaced **[HOUSE]**.

## 5. Common mistakes
- Reposting other brands' or creators' videos (originality demotion + copyright).
- Posting only static price cards.
- Answering price questions with "inbox" only (users dislike it; answer publicly in short).
- Engagement bait ("comment YES") — Meta has long demoted engagement bait **[COMMUNITY — widely reported]**.
