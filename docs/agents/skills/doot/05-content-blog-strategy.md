## 1. What Google rewards and punishes (official)

- Google judges **quality, not how content is produced**: "Our focus on the quality of content, rather than how content is produced" (Search Quality team, Feb 2023). AI use is allowed; AI used mainly to manipulate rankings is spam **[OFFICIAL, via Search Engine Land report]**.
- **Who / How / Why:** make authorship clear; explain how content was made (tests, photos, measurements; disclose substantial AI generation where readers would wonder); the main purpose must be to help people **[OFFICIAL framework, via SEL + Page One Power]**.
- E-E-A-T: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, trust — **trust matters most**; first-hand experience counts (we make and sell these garments — show it).
- **Scaled content abuse** (spam policy): "Using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users" → manual action risk **[OFFICIAL]**. Blueprint already says: quality over volume; mass thin AI content is penalised.
- **AI Overviews / AI Mode:** "There are no additional requirements" — be indexed and snippet-eligible; keep important content in text; structured data must match visible text; **no special AI text files or schema are needed** for Google **[OFFICIAL]**. Clicks from AI features are counted in Search Console → Performance → Web.

## 2. EXTAS content pillars (priority order from blueprint C4.5, refined)

| Pillar | Example articles (EN + BN titles) | Links to | Win condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Buying help** | How to choose your polo size (height/weight chart) · পোলো শার্টের সাইজ কিভাবে বুঝবেন · Polo vs T-shirt: which for office? · Best fabrics for Bangladesh summer · GSM explained | category + size guide | fewer size refusals; ranks for informational queries |
| **Care & problem-solving** | How to stop a collar curling · Washing white tees without yellowing · Hoodie pilling: why and how to prevent | relevant PDPs | evergreen traffic, trust |
| **Style & occasion** | Eid outfit ideas for men (combos) · Winter layering guide · 5 ways to wear a navy polo | combo pages | seasonal demand |
| **Proof & brand** | How we make our pique polo (fabric, stitching, QC — with real factory/QC photos) · Washed 10 times: our test | brand + PDPs | E-E-A-T, link-worthy |
| **Local data** (link-earning) | "Bangladesh men's size report" from our own anonymised size/fit data · Winter buying trends by district (from our orders, aggregated) | home/category | press links (D07) |

Rules **[HOUSE + OFFICIAL basis]**:
1. Every money article has a product/combo block and links to the pillar category with a descriptive anchor.
2. Topic clusters: one pillar page per category (the category page or a guide), supporting articles linking to it and to each other where relevant (blueprint: cluster planner with internal-link enforcement).
3. Cadence: quality over volume — start with **1–2 excellent articles/week**, not 20 thin ones **[HOUSE]**. Blueprint's "3–4 a week" is an upper target once quality gates are stable.
4. Real evidence in every article: own photos, measurements, tests, staff/customer quotes (with consent). Byline: a named EXTAS person with a short bio (Who).
5. AI may draft; a human (or Doot under owner-approved rules) checks every fact against product data and policy (Smart Rules). Never invent statistics, reviews or testimonials.
6. Bengali: write natively in চলতি বাংলা (not machine-translated English) for the `/bn/` versions or Bengali-first articles.

## 3. Article template (quotable shape — helps users, snippets and AI answers)

```
H1: the question or task in the reader's words
Lead (40–60 words): the direct answer first
Table or steps (size chart, comparison)
Sections with question-style H2s
"How we know": test/photo/measurement evidence
Product block (live price/stock)
FAQ (3–5 real customer questions from chats/search logs)
Byline + date updated
```

## 4. Content production workflow
1. Topic from the keyword map / customer chats / search logs (D03).
2. Brief (intent, target query, outline, evidence needed, internal links, CTA).
3. Draft → fact check (product data, policies) → Bengali/English proof → SEO check (title, H1, links, schema) → publish.
4. Distribute: Facebook/Instagram post or reel (D08), WhatsApp broadcast only to opted-in customers within message limits (D09).
5. Review at 28 and 90 days in Search Console: impressions, position, clicks, assisted orders. Update or merge underperformers; never leave dozens of zero-impression thin posts.

## 5. YouTube (blueprint C4.4)
- Per product: short clip (fabric close-up, fit on model, movement). Evergreen: "how to measure your size", "polo vs t-shirt", styling.
- Embed on the PDP with VideoObject markup; description links to the product with a tracked URL.
- Titles in the language people search; Bengali captions.
- Shorts double as Reels/TikTok (D08) — one shoot, three platforms.

## 6. AI assistants (answer-engine optimisation) — honest version
- For Google's AI features: normal SEO is the method (official). For other assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude…): being crawlable (AI-crawler switch default **allow**), consistent facts across our site, Facebook page, marketplaces and press, and quotable factual statements are the reasonable levers **[COMMUNITY — test before use]**.
- `llms.txt`-style summaries: Google says they're not needed for Search; harmless to keep as a low-priority item; no evidence of ranking effect **[OFFICIAL for Google; unknown for others]**.
- Track AI-assistant referrals separately in analytics (blueprint).

## 7. Content quality gate (must pass before publish)
- [ ] Answers the query better than the current top 3 results (checked).
- [ ] Contains first-hand evidence (photo/test/measurement) or our own data.
- [ ] Facts match product data and Smart Rules today.
- [ ] No invented numbers, reviews or quotes.
- [ ] Byline + updated date.
- [ ] Internal links: pillar + 1–3 products/combos.
- [ ] Not a near-duplicate of an existing EXTAS page.
