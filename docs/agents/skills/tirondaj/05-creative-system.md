# T05 · Creative system for menswear ads — hooks, UGC, combo offers, Bengali/English copy, specs per placement

*Tirondaj তীরন্দাজ · A360-07 · module 5 of 9 · level: Post-graduate · version 1.0 · as of October 2026*
*Works with Shilpi (photo standard, skills/shilpi/photo/) and the studio (S24 virtual photoshoot, S25 video, PR 270 creative briefs).*

**Why this is the biggest lever:** Meta says its delivery system now finds audiences through creative ("from niche
targeting to creative diversification"); TikTok says big creative differences work better in testing. On both, the
creative *is* the targeting. A polo ad with a gym-guy hook reaches different people than the same polo with an
office hook.

---

## 1. The concept matrix — every new ad is one cell

| Axis | Values for EXTAS |
|---|---|
| **Persona** | student (18–24), young office worker, gym/sport, groom-side Eid buyer, gift buyer (sister/wife buying for him), outside-Dhaka value buyer |
| **Angle** | price/combo saving · fabric quality proof · fit/size confidence · season (winter warmth, Eid look) · trust (COD, check-at-door, exchange) · social proof (real reviews, real customers) |
| **Format** | UGC talking head · try-on transition · flat-lay combo reveal · on-model lookbook reel · catalogue carousel/collection · static price card · creator Spark Ad |
| **Hook** (first 1–3 s) | see §2 |

Rule: a test batch of 5 must differ on at least **two axes** each. Same persona + same angle + new colour = not a new concept.

## 2. Hook bank (first 1–3 seconds) — Bengali first, English variant

| # | Hook (on-screen + voice) | Type |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | "৩টা পোলো ১,৯৯৯ টাকায় — সত্যি?" / "3 polos for ৳1,999 — really?" | price shock (only with the real current price) |
| H2 | "এই হুডির ভিতরটা দেখুন" + macro of fleece | proof |
| H3 | "৫'৮", ৭০ কেজি — আমি L পরি" | size confidence |
| H4 | "অফিস থেকে আড্ডা — এক কম্বোতেই" | use case |
| H5 | "১০ বার ধোয়ার পরে" (side-by-side) | durability proof |
| H6 | Parcel opened at the door: "আগে দেখুন, তারপর টাকা" | trust (only if policy allows checking at door) |
| H7 | "ঈদের আগে শেষ ডেলিভারি: <date>" | urgency (date from Bhandari's courier cut-off) |
| H8 | "শীতে ৩টা জিনিস লাগবেই" → hoodie, jogger, cap | listicle |

Hook rules: offer/price visible by second 3 (TikTok: proposition in first 3 s); text 5–10 words/second; human face or hand in frame early; no slow logo intro.

## 3. UGC system

1. **Sources:** real customers (review-with-photo reward: a coupon on the next order, never cash-for-praise scripts), micro-creators (paid, contract with usage rights and Spark authorization length), staff try-ons.
2. **Brief template (one page):** persona · angle · hook line (Bengali) · 3 must-show shots (fabric macro, full-body fit, price card) · banned claims · length 15–30 s · 9:16 · natural light · voice in চলতি বাংলা.
3. **Honesty rules:** no invented reviews (EXTAS standing rule — "no generator that writes customer reviews out of nothing"); paid creators disclose partnership where platform tools exist (branded content toggle); all prices and discounts must exist on the site at the time the ad runs.

## 4. Combo / bundle offers in ads

- Lead with the **set**, not the piece: the set price, the saving in taka ("আলাদা কিনলে ৳2,350 — কম্বোতে ৳1,999, বাঁচবে ৳351"), and the photo showing all pieces together (Shilpi P04 set composition).
- Tiered sets: "২ পিস ৳1,399 · ৩ পিস ৳1,999" — a 3-tier ladder lets the middle option look reasonable; show the per-piece price for the biggest set.
- Delivery maths in the ad when it helps: free delivery threshold or "একই ডেলিভারি চার্জে ৩ পিস".
- Catalogue: combos are their own catalogue items (T01 §3) so dynamic ads can show a set to someone who viewed one piece.
- Never take a customer from a combo ad to a single-product page (owner rule, lessons/ALL.md) — link combos to the combo page.

## 5. Copy rules (Bengali / English)

- Primary language **Bengali (চলতি, simple words)**; English for product names and sizes ("Pique Polo", "L", "XL"). Test a Banglish variant on TikTok.
- Structure of primary text (Meta): line 1 hook (fits before "See more"), line 2 offer + price, line 3 trust (COD · exchange · delivery time), line 4 CTA.
- Example (Meta feed):
  > শীতের কম্বো রেডি 🔥 হুডি + জগার মাত্র ৳2,190
  > ভেতরে নরম ফ্লিস, সাইজ M–3XL
  > ক্যাশ অন ডেলিভারি · ঢাকায় ৳60, বাইরে ৳120 · ৭ দিনে এক্সচেঞ্জ
  > এখনই অর্ডার করুন 👇
  (prices and policy words are placeholders — always pulled from the live site settings, never typed by hand)
- Character guidance (secondary source, Strike Social 2026): Facebook feed image primary text ~80 and headline ~27 before truncation; Instagram feed ~125; Reels primary text ~40–72. Put the essentials in the first 40 characters.
- Banned: "100% guaranteed", fake "was" prices, "last chance" when it is not, health claims about fabric, competitor names.

## 6. Specs per placement (build every concept in 3 ratios)

| Placement | Ratio | Pixels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta FB/IG feed | **4:5** | 1080×1350 | main asset for feed |
| Meta Stories / Reels | **9:16** | 1080×1920 | Reels: keep ~14% top, ~35% bottom, ~6% sides clear; Stories: ~14% top, ~20% bottom (secondary source) |
| Meta carousel / Marketplace | 1:1 | 1080×1080 | all cards same ratio |
| Meta right column / landscape link | 1.91:1 | 1200×628 | low priority |
| Meta files | — | — | JPG/PNG ≤ 30 MB; MP4/MOV H.264 ≤ 4 GB |
| Meta catalogue image | ≥ 500×500 | 1080×1080 or 1080×1350 | catalogue `image_link` JPEG/PNG ≤ 8 MB |
| TikTok in-feed | **9:16** | 1080×1920 (≥ 720p) | sound on; safe zone centre |
| Google Demand Gen | 9:16, 1:1, 16:9 video; vertical, square, horizontal images | ≥ 3 images of each shape + 1 video of each shape per campaign | Google's own recommendation |
| Google Merchant Center main image | white/transparent background | ≥ 512×512, ideally ≥ 1024; width 1500 recommended | clothing on model; no head/feet crop in full-body |
| YouTube in-stream | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | cut from the same shoot |

Shoot rule for Shilpi/studio: frame for **9:16 with the garment inside the central 4:5 area** so one shoot crops to 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 without losing the product.

## 7. Creative testing cadence and scoring

- **Volume:** 3–5 new concepts per week into the test lab (T01 §6); refresh winners' variants monthly.
- **Leading indicators** (first 1,000–3,000 impressions): thumb-stop rate (3-s views ÷ impressions) for video, link CTR, cost per landing-page view.
- **Deciding indicator:** cost per confirmed order, then delivered CPA after 7–10 days.
- **Creative scorecard** stored per ad: concept cell, hook ID, spend, confirmed orders, delivered orders, delivered CPA, refusal rate, fatigue date. Patterns in this table (e.g. "size-confidence hooks have lower refusal") become proposed lessons.

## 8. Checklist before any creative goes live

- [ ] Price, combo contents, delivery charge, exchange rule = live site values today.
- [ ] Offer visible within 3 s (video) / readable at thumbnail size (static).
- [ ] 9:16, 4:5, 1:1 versions exist; text inside safe zones.
- [ ] Colour of garment matches the real product (Shilpi colour check) — wrong colour = refusals.
- [ ] Bengali proof-read (চলতি); English product name correct.
- [ ] Links: combo ad → combo page; UTM auto-wired.
- [ ] No unapproved claims, no invented reviews.
