Season rules **[HOUSE]**:
1. Season asset groups/ads uploaded **≥ 2 weeks before** the peak (Google's PMax guidance) and launched ≥ 7 days before the rush so learning finishes before the money days.
2. One season proposal to the owner (T07 §7). No budget step without approval.
3. The day after the cut-off: stop selling what can't arrive; switch creative to "ঈদের পরে ডেলিভারি" only if the owner wants post-Eid orders.

## 2. Paying for ads from Bangladesh (USD)

Facts found (each with date — rules change, verify with the bank before each season):
- Ad platforms bill in USD; the cost per $1 includes the bank rate, card fees and VAT. The Daily Star (Bangla), Nov 2022: cost per $1 rose from ৳105–110 to ৳120–125 **after 15% VAT**, and to ৳135–140 through third parties without a BIN.
- Bangladesh Bank instructed banks (circular reported 5 March 2019) to collect **15% VAT** on payments to Facebook, YouTube, Google etc. by card, TT or any mode. Google obtained Bangladesh VAT registration in May 2021; Facebook was "in process" then. The current VAT mechanism on each invoice: **verify with the accountant**.
- April 2023 (TBS): Meta's BD revenue agent (Httpool) warned ad services might be "unavailable or limited" during the dollar crisis — **supply of dollars can stop ads**. Keep a backup payment method and don't plan a season on one card.
- June 2025 (Daily Star): Bangladesh Bank circular lets local advertisers pay for Google/Facebook ads **through local advertising agencies** without separate central-bank approval, with documentation (agreements, invoices, tax deductions).
- Card limits: banks set USD limits on international/dual-currency cards; the specific current limit was **not found** in a reliable source. Owner records his card's monthly USD limit in the dashboard; the monthly ceiling can never exceed it.

Rules **[HOUSE]**:
1. The owner's USD rate (PR 266) must include bank charges + VAT — use the true "taka per ad dollar", not the market rate.
2. Before a season: confirm card limit headroom ≥ season plan + 20%; set up the backup (second card or agency route).
3. A failed payment pauses all Meta ads at once → alert immediately; this is a sales emergency, not a routine notice.

## 3. COD refusals — what we know and don't

- e-CAB research (cited by the US ITA Country Commercial Guide, updated Mar 2026): **> 90% of e-commerce users prefer COD**.
- Refusal / return-to-origin rate in Bangladesh: no reliable published figure found. Figures like "20–40% RTO" (eGrow) and "social-ad COD orders have double the RTO" (EasySell) have **no sources** and come from vendors selling COD tools → **do not quote**. Use **our own** delivered/refused data by channel, campaign and district.
- What reduces refusals (2-source or official-level support is thin; treat as **[COMMUNITY — test before use]** unless marked):
  - Order confirmation call/SMS/WhatsApp soon after the order (EXTAS already confirms orders).
  - **Advance delivery charge by bKash for high-risk orders** — EXTAS blueprint's decision (fraud engine, Gyani owns) **[HOUSE]**.
  - Accurate colour and size info in ads (wrong expectation → refusal) **[HOUSE, from blueprint]**.
  - Faster delivery (impulse fades) — reasoning, unproven.
  - Excluding past refusers from ads **[HOUSE]**.

## 4. Dhaka vs outside Dhaka

- Delivery charge (from checkout settings): Dhaka city ৳60, outside ৳120–130. Delivery takes longer outside Dhaka → delivered events arrive later (T02 §4).
- Default targeting: **all Bangladesh**, let the algorithm find buyers; then apply **value rules** by region/city using 60–90 days of *our* delivered ROAS by district (T01 §4).
- Separate Dhaka/outside campaigns only when a creative/offer genuinely differs (e.g. "ঢাকায় ২৪ ঘণ্টায় ডেলিভারি") — and only if both still get enough events to exit learning.
- Watch the per-district table in the ads dashboard (EXTAS blueprint: sales by district/thana with return rate per area). A district with refusal far above average: lower its value, don't exclude outright on one bad week.

## 5. Channel habits

- Facebook is the most used social platform in Bangladesh (DHL guide citing The Daily Star, Oct 2024); ~60 million active social media users in Jan 2025 (DataReportal, via DHL).
- Many buyers message first (Messenger/WhatsApp) before ordering — click-to-WhatsApp/Messenger ads can work, but the chat must turn into an order with source tracking (EXTAS chat-to-order panel) or they are unmeasurable. Optimise such campaigns only when chat orders carry the ad ID into our DB.
- Language: চলতি বাংলা primary; English product names; Banglish appears in search.
