# Tirondaj · Doesn't work / myths (paid ads)

*Each line: the myth → what the evidence says → what Tirondaj does instead. As of October 2026.*

| # | Myth | Evidence | Do instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Never change budget more than 20% or learning resets." | No Meta rule gives a %; Meta only says big changes (e.g. 10×) may restart learning while tiny ones won't (Jon Loomer quoting Meta). The 20% figure is practitioner lore. | Steps of 15–20% every 2–3 days are a *cautious habit* **[COMMUNITY]**, not law; measure marginal dCPA (T09 P7). |
| 2 | "Max 6 ads per ad set." | Meta removed that guidance (Jon Loomer); hard limit is 50 ads. | 6–15 distinct concepts; diversity matters more than count. |
| 3 | "More interest-targeted ad sets = more control = better." | Meta's own direction is broad + creative diversification (Andromeda statement); many small ad sets → "Learning limited". | Consolidate (T01 §2). |
| 4 | "Optimise to Purchase on the thank-you page — that's the standard." | For COD it teaches the system to find people who order and refuse (EXTAS blueprint C6.2; lesson in lessons/tirondaj.md). | Optimise toward confirmed/delivered (T02 §4). |
| 5 | "Ads Manager ROAS 4 = we're profitable." | ROAS ignores refusals, COGS, courier, VAT and bank charges. | Judge on dCPA and contribution margin (T06). |
| 6 | "Retargeting is our best campaign (ROAS 10!)." | Retargeting and brand are the least incremental (Seer test; r/PPC brand thread). | Cap retargeting share; test with a holdout. |
| 7 | "PMax should run on everything including brand." | Brand traffic inflates PMax results and steals cheap conversions (SEL; r/PPC consensus). | Brand exclusions in PMax; separate brand Search. |
| 8 | "Boost post is the same as an Ads Manager campaign." | Boost uses limited objectives and settings; it can't use our delivered-purchase optimisation or exclusion structure (Meta API: Advantage+ Sales needs the three levers). | Run sales through Ads Manager campaigns only. |
| 9 | "Exclusions are a hard wall." | Dollar Commerce reports prospecting can still spend 30–40% on existing customers despite exclusions. | Check new/existing breakdown weekly. |
| 10 | "Frequency above 3 means fatigue." | No official threshold; Meta's fatigue flag is based on cost per result vs history (Jon Loomer). | Watch CPA + CTR trend, not frequency alone. |
| 11 | "COD refusal in BD is 30% — everybody knows." | No reliable published source; vendor blogs give unsourced ranges. | Use our own refused % by channel/district. |
| 12 | "Video Action Campaigns for YouTube conversions." | VAC retired; upgraded to Demand Gen (Google, 2025–2026). | Use Demand Gen. |
| 13 | "Duplicate a winning ad set to scale." | Duplicates restart learning and compete in the same auction; community split on it (r/FacebookAds). | Raise the winner's budget in steps or add the concept to the main campaign. **[COMMUNITY]** |
| 14 | "Set tROAS on order value." | Refused orders inflate value; Google uses the values you send. | Upload retractions/restatements or set target on delivered value (T03 §5–6). |
| 15 | "Separate testing campaigns are always required." | Community disagreement; with small budgets a separate lab may starve both (r/FacebookAds). | Small lab (≤ 15% of spend) while budget is small; review at higher spend. **[COMMUNITY]** |
| 16 | "Verification/IVR/SMS confirmation cuts RTO by 34–41% (or 20–35%, or 25–40%)." | Indian vendor blogs give these with no method and contradict themselves (T10 §4). | Confirmation is a [2-SRC] practice; the effect size is ours to measure. |
| 17 | "Block COD for high-refusal regions to fix RTO." | Razorpay and Shipmozo (India) warn static region/pincode blocks reject genuine buyers; EXTAS forbids auto-blocking. | Risk-tiered friction (advance delivery charge for high risk), value rules by district (T08, T10). |
| 18 | "Say 'Free COD, no questions asked' — it lifts CTR." | Indian and Pakistani practitioners report vague COD promises attract refusers; ad, page and policy must match (T10 §3). | State the real COD/exchange terms in the ad. |

Sources: see T01, T03, T06, T09, T10 source lists (Jon Loomer pages, Meta Engineering Andromeda, Seer Interactive, Search Engine Land PMax brand exclusions, Dollar Commerce, Google VAC upgrade page, r/PPC and r/FacebookAds threads).
