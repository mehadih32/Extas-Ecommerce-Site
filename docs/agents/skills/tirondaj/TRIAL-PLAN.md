# Tirondaj · Trial plan — one honest free trial per tool, taken by the owner, at the right time

*PR 585 · as of October 2026*

**Rules:** one trial per tool, the owner's own account and card, no repeat trials, no throwaway accounts. Start a trial
only when (a) the site is live with ≥ 4 weeks of our own data, (b) Tirondaj has the extraction checklist below ready,
(c) the owner has a reminder set to cancel before day 7 if not buying. Export everything to the shared drive on day 6.

## Timing order
1. **Foreplay** — first, 3–4 weeks before the next big season (Eid or winter), when competitors are launching season creative.
2. **Similarweb** — once, after 8+ weeks live (needs our own traffic to compare).
3. **Semrush** — coordinate with Doot: one trial serves both agents (Doot leads the SEO half; Tirondaj takes the PPC half on days 1–2).
